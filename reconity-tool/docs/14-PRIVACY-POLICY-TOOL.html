Privacy Policy

Reconity Security Platform • Application/Tool

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy details how Reconity Technologies handles sensitive security data, account information, and scan results within the Reconity Tool.

2. Data We Process

  • Account Credentials: Hashed passwords and session tokens.
  • Target Domains: The infrastructure assets you submit for scanning.
  • Scan Results: Vulnerabilities, subdomains, and technical findings.
  • Access Logs: Record of system interactions for security auditing.

3. Purpose of Processing

  • To provide automated security assessments.
  • To maintain historical scan reports for your review.
  • To comply with legal obligations in Nepal (ETA 2063).

4. Security Controls

Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Access is restricted via role-based permissions.

5. Data Retention

Scan data is retained for 12 months or until account deletion. See our full Data Retention Policy for details.
