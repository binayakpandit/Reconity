1. Getting Started

1.1 Creating an Account

Step 1: Navigate to the Reconity login page at https://reconity.tech
Step 2: Click "Register" and enter your email address and a strong password
Step 3: Verify your email (if required) and log in

1.2 Logging In

Enter your registered email and password on the login page. Upon successful authentication, you will be redirected to the Dashboard.

💡 Tip: Your session remains active for 24 hours. You'll be automatically redirected to the dashboard if you're already logged in.

2. Dashboard Overview

The Dashboard provides a high-level view of your security posture:

  • Total Targets: Number of domains you're monitoring
  • Active Scans: Scans currently in progress
  • Last Scan: Most recent scan activity

Use the sidebar to navigate between sections: Dashboard, Targets, and Reports.

3. Managing Targets

3.1 Adding a Target

Step 1: Click "Targets" in the sidebar
Step 2: Enter the domain name (e.g., example.com) in the input field
Step 3: Click "Add Target"
⚠️ Important: Only add domains you own or have explicit authorization to scan. Unauthorized scanning is illegal.

3.2 Viewing Targets

All added targets appear in a list showing the domain name and current status (IDLE, QUEUED, SCANNING, COMPLETED, or FAILED).

4. Running Scans

4.1 Quick Scan (OSINT)

Quick Scan uses Certificate Transparency logs to discover subdomains passively.

How to Run: Click the "Quick Scan" button next to any target. Results appear immediately.

Benefits: Fast, non-intrusive, leaves no trace on target infrastructure.

4.2 Full Scan (Active)

Full Scan performs deep DNS enumeration using multiple sources and tools.

How to Run: Click the "Full Scan" button. The scan is queued and processed in the background.

Duration: Full scans may take several minutes depending on the domain size.

💡 Tip: Start with a Quick Scan for immediate results, then run a Full Scan for comprehensive coverage.

5. Viewing Reports

5.1 Accessing Reports

After a scan completes:

Step 1: Go to the Targets page
Step 2: Find the target with "COMPLETED" status
Step 3: Click "View Report" to see detailed findings

5.2 Report Contents

Each report includes:

  • Executive Summary: High-level overview with risk score
  • Asset Inventory: Total subdomains discovered
  • Technical Details: Complete list of discovered subdomains

5.3 Exporting Reports

Click the "Export PDF" button to generate a print-friendly report suitable for sharing with stakeholders.

6. Account Settings

6.1 Logging Out

Click "Logout" at the bottom of the sidebar to securely end your session.

6.2 Password Security

  • Use a strong, unique password (12+ characters recommended)
  • Never share your credentials
  • Contact support if you suspect unauthorized access
