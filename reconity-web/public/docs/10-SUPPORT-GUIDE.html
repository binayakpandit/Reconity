3. Before Contacting Support

To help us resolve your issue faster, please:

  • Check the User Manual for guidance
  • Try logging out and back in
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
  • Check the GitHub Issues page for known issues

4. What to Include in Your Support Request

  • Email: The email associated with your account
  • Description: Clear explanation of the issue
  • Steps to Reproduce: How can we recreate the problem?
  • Expected Behavior: What should have happened?
  • Actual Behavior: What actually happened?
  • Browser & OS: e.g., Chrome 120 on Windows 11
  • Screenshots: If applicable, attach images showing the issue

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: My scan is stuck in "QUEUED" status. What should I do?

A: Background scans may take time during high-traffic periods. If a scan remains queued for more than 30 minutes, contact support with the target domain.

Q: I forgot my password. How can I reset it?

A: Contact support@reconity.tech with your registered email address to request a password reset.

Q: Why are some subdomains not showing in my scan results?

A: Quick Scans only use Certificate Transparency logs. For comprehensive results, run a Full Scan which uses multiple discovery methods.

Q: Can I delete scan results?

A: Data deletion can be requested by contacting privacy@reconity.tech per our Data Retention Policy.

Q: Is my data secure?

A: Yes. We use industry-standard encryption, secure authentication, and follow best practices. See our Privacy Policy for details.
