Data Protection & Retention Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Compliant with GDPR & CCPA

1. Purpose & Scope

This policy establishes guidelines for the protection, retention, and disposal of data collected and processed by Reconity Technologies. It applies to all employees, contractors, and systems handling personal and organizational data.

2. Data Classification

Classification Description Examples Protection Level
Public Information intended for public access Marketing materials, public docs Standard
Internal Operational data not for external sharing Internal reports, policies Moderate
Confidential Sensitive business or personal data User emails, scan results High
Restricted Highly sensitive data requiring strictest controls Passwords, API keys, vulnerability details Critical

3. Data Retention Schedule

Data Type Retention Period Justification Disposal Method
User Account Data Duration of account + 30 days Account recovery grace period Database deletion
Scan Results 12 months Historical analysis & compliance Automated purge
Access Logs 90 days Security monitoring Log rotation
Authentication Tokens 24 hours Session security Automatic expiry
Payment Records 7 years Legal/tax requirements Secure archive
Support Tickets 3 years Service improvement Anonymization

4. Data Protection Measures

4.1 Technical Controls

  • Encryption at Rest: Database encryption using AES-256
  • Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.3 for all network communications
  • Access Control: Role-based permissions with principle of least privilege
  • Audit Logging: All data access logged and monitored

4.2 Organizational Controls

  • Security awareness training for all personnel
  • Background checks for employees with data access
  • Incident response procedures documented and tested
  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing

5. Data Subject Rights (GDPR Compliance)

Response Time: We respond to all data subject requests within 30 days as required by GDPR Article 12.
  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Users may request copies of their data
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Users may correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17): Users may request data deletion
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Users may export data in JSON format
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Users may object to certain processing

6. Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach:

  • Affected users notified within 72 hours of discovery
  • Relevant supervisory authorities notified as required by law
  • Incident documented with root cause analysis
  • Remediation measures implemented and communicated

7. International Data Transfers

When transferring data outside the European Economic Area (EEA), we ensure adequate protection through:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Binding Corporate Rules where applicable
  • Adequacy decisions by the European Commission
