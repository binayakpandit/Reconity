Regulatory Compliance & Legal Framework

1. Regulatory Landscape

Reconity operates within a complex global regulatory environment with particular focus on Nepal's legal framework. This document outlines our compliance strategy across key jurisdictions.

2. Nepal Legal Framework 🇳🇵

As a Nepal-based cybersecurity platform, Reconity is subject to and complies with the following Nepali laws and regulations:

🏛️ Constitution of Nepal 2015

Article 27 guarantees the fundamental right to privacy for all citizens. This forms the constitutional basis for all data protection in Nepal.

2.1 Electronic Transaction Act 2063 (2006/2008)

The ETA 2063 is the foundational law governing cyber activities in Nepal. It provides:

  • Legal recognition of electronic records and digital signatures
  • Framework for secure electronic transactions
  • Definitions and penalties for cyber offenses including:
    • Section 47: Unauthorized access (hacking) - up to 3 years imprisonment or NPR 200,000 fine
    • Spreading viruses/malware - criminal penalties
    • Publishing illegal electronic content - up to 5 years or NPR 100,000 fine
⚠️ User Responsibility: Users must only scan assets they own or have written authorization to test. Unauthorized scanning violates ETA Section 47 and may result in criminal prosecution.

2.2 Individual Privacy Act 2018 (2075)

The Individual Privacy Act is Nepal's primary privacy statute establishing:

  • Recognition of individual right to privacy
  • Consent requirements for personal data collection
  • Obligations for public bodies and data handlers
  • Penalties for unauthorized disclosure of personal information

2.3 Individual Privacy Regulation 2020 (2077)

Provides implementation guidance for the Privacy Act of 2018.

2.4 Data Act 2079 (2022)

Enacted October 2022, this Act regulates:

  • Generation, storage, and publication of data
  • Obligations for data controllers, producers, and users
  • Framework for data governance in Nepal

2.5 Muluki Penal Code 2074 (2017)

Criminalizes privacy violations including:

  • Unauthorized collection or disclosure of personal data
  • Unauthorized recording of conversations
  • Photo/video tampering and misuse
  • Electronic harassment

2.6 National Cyber Security Policy 2023 (2080)

Approved August 2023 by the Nepal Cabinet, this policy establishes:

  • Strategic roadmap for protecting digital infrastructure
  • Information and data security standards
  • Targets for improving Nepal's Global Cyber Security Index score
  • Public awareness and capacity building initiatives

2.7 NTA Cybersecurity Bye-Law 2077 (2020)

Issued by Nepal Telecommunications Authority (NTA), mandating:

  • Cybersecurity standards for ISPs and telecom providers
  • Regular security audits
  • Incident response team requirements
  • Data protection measures

4. Legal Use of Scanning Tools

⚠️ Critical: Under Nepal's Electronic Transaction Act 2063 (Section 47) and Muluki Penal Code 2074, unauthorized access to computer systems is a criminal offense punishable by imprisonment and fines.

Reconity requires users to:

  • Confirm ownership or written authorization before adding targets
  • Accept Terms of Service acknowledging legal responsibilities
  • Maintain documentation of authorization for enterprise accounts
  • Only scan domains within their legal scope

5. Anti-Abuse Measures

  • Rate limiting on API endpoints to prevent scanning abuse
  • Logging of all scan requests with user attribution
  • Automated detection of suspicious scanning patterns
  • Cooperation with Nepal Police Cyber Bureau upon valid legal process

6. Dispute Resolution

Disputes arising from use of the Service shall be resolved through arbitration in accordance with the rules of the Nepal Arbitration Council, with the seat of arbitration in Kathmandu, Nepal. The governing law shall be the laws of the Federal Democratic Republic of Nepal.
