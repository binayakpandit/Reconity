🤖 AI Guidelines

Principles for Responsible AI Use in Cybersecurity

1. Introduction

Reconity leverages artificial intelligence to enhance cybersecurity operations, from automated vulnerability detection to intelligent report generation. This document outlines our commitment to responsible, ethical, and transparent AI use.

2. Core Principles

🎯 Accuracy & Reliability

All AI-generated findings are validated against known databases and cross-referenced with multiple sources. We clearly distinguish between AI-assisted insights and verified facts.

🔍 Transparency

Users are informed when AI is involved in generating content, recommendations, or analysis. AI-generated reports are clearly labeled.

👤 Human Oversight

AI assists human operators but does not make autonomous decisions with significant impact. Critical security decisions require human approval.

🔐 Privacy Preservation

AI models are trained on anonymized data. User-specific data is never used to train shared models without explicit consent.

⚖️ Fairness & Non-Discrimination

AI systems are designed to avoid bias. We regularly audit for discriminatory patterns in automated decisions.

🛡️ Security

AI systems are protected against adversarial attacks, prompt injection, and model manipulation.

3. AI Applications in Reconity

  • Vulnerability Correlation: AI matches discovered assets against CVE databases and prioritizes by exploitability
  • Report Generation: Natural language summaries of technical findings for executive audiences
  • Threat Intelligence: Pattern recognition across threat feeds to identify emerging attack vectors
  • Learning Assistance: Personalized learning paths in Cyber Odyssey RPG based on skill progression
  • Code Development: Antigravity AI assistant for platform development (clearly documented)

4. AI Development Partners

Reconity was developed with assistance from:

  • Antigravity (Google DeepMind) - Agentic AI coding assistant for development, debugging, and documentation

All AI-generated code is reviewed by human developers before deployment.

5. Data Handling for AI

  • User data is never shared with third-party AI providers without encryption and anonymization
  • AI models used for scanning do not retain user-specific information after processing
  • Training data sources are documented and auditable

6. Limitations & Disclaimers

Users should be aware that:

  • AI-generated vulnerability assessments may contain false positives or miss unknown vulnerabilities
  • AI risk scores are indicators, not guarantees of exploitability
  • AI cannot replace professional security assessment for high-stakes environments

7. Feedback & Reporting

To report concerns about AI behavior, bias, or accuracy, contact: ai-ethics@reconity.tech
